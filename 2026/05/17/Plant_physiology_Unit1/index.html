Unit 1

Plant and Cell Architecture

1. Plant Cell Structures and Organelles

  • Primary wall
  • Secondary wall
  • Middle lamella(胞间层)
  • Intercellular air space (细胞间气腔)
    • Cellulose(纤维素)
    • Cellulose microfibrils(纤维素微纤维)
    • Hemicellulose(半纤维素)
    • Pectin(果胶)
    • Lignin(木质素)
  • Plasma membrane(质膜)
    • Phospholipid bilayer, integral protein, peripheral protein, glycoprotein(磷脂双分子层，整体蛋白，外周蛋白，糖蛋白)
  • Cytosol(细胞溶质)
  • Cytoplasm(细胞质)
  • Plasmodesmata(胞间连丝)
  • Organelle(细胞器)
    • Vacuole, Tonoplast(液泡，液泡层)
    • Nucleus, nuclear envelope, nucleolus, chromatin(核，核膜，核仁，染色质)
    • Endoplasmic reticulum(内质网)
    • smooth vs. rough, ribosome, oil bodies(光滑与粗糙，核糖体，油体)
  • Golgi apparatus(高尔基体)
  • Mitochondria, outer and inner membrane(线粒体，外膜和内膜)
  • Chloroplast, outer and inner membrane, thylakoid, thylakoid membrane, thylakoid lumen, (叶绿体，内外膜，类囊体，类囊体膜，类囊体腔)
  • granum, stroma lamellae, stroma(基粒，基质片层，基质)
  • Chromoplast, amyloplast(有色体，造粉体)
  • Microbodies, peroxisome(微体，过氧化物酶体)
  • Cytoskeleton, cytoplasmic streaming(细胞骨架，胞质环流)
  • Symplast vs. Apoplast(共质体，质外体)

2. General Plant Structures (organs, tissues, cells)

  • Monocot vs. Dicot (differences in tissue arrangement in stem, roots, and leaves)
  • Organs
    • Stem
    • Root, lateral root (根，侧根)
    • Leaf blade, petiole(叶片，叶柄)
    • Nodes
    • Internodes
  • Meristems(分生组织)
    • Primary vs. secondary growth
    • Apical (shoot and root)(顶端分生组织，茎尖与根尖)
    • Axillary(腋生分生组织)
    • Intercalary(居间分生组织)
    • Lateral(侧生分生组织)
    • Vascular cambium(维管形成层)
  • Tissues
    • Dermal
      • Epidermis, trichomes(表皮，表皮毛)
  • Ground
    • Parenchyma(薄壁组织)
    • Collenchyma(厚角组织)
    • Sclerenchyma(厚壁组织)
  • Vascular
  • Xylem, tracheids, vessel elements, vessel (木质部，管胞，导管分子，导管)
  • Phloem, sieve tube elements, sieve tube, sieve plates, companion cell(韧皮部，筛管分子，筛管，筛板，伴胞)
  • Cortex(皮层)
  • Pith(髓)
  • Vascular cambium(维管形成层)
  • Vascular bundle(维管束)
  • Pericycle(中柱鞘)
  • Endodermis(内皮层)
  • Root hair(根毛)
  • Secondary xylem and phloem(次生木质部与次生韧皮部)
  • Heartwood and sapwood(心材，边材)
  • Rays(维管射线)
  • Cuticle(角质层)
  • Mesophyll(叶肉)
  • Spongy mesophyll(海绵组织)
  • Palisade parenchyma(栅栏组织)
  • Bundle sheath cells(维管束鞘细胞)
  • Stomata, guard cells(气孔，保卫细胞)

3. Seed and Seedling structures

  • Zones in a meristem (such as a root apical meristem)(分生组织分区，如根尖分生组织)
  • Meristematic zone(分生区)
  • Elongation zone(伸长区)
  • Maturation zone(成熟区)

Non-endospermic vs. endospermic seeds(无胚乳种子，有胚乳种子)

Seed parts:

  • Testa or seed coat(种皮)

  • Endosperm(胚乳)

  • Hypocotyl(下胚轴)

  • Epicotyl(上胚轴)

  • Radicle(胚根)

  • Plumule(胚芽)

  • Scutellum(盾片)

  • Coleoptile(胚芽鞘)

  • Coleorhiza(胚根鞘)

  • Hypogeal germination(留土萌发)

  • Epigeal germination(出土萌发)

  1. What is the function of the pericycle?

meristem that gives rise to branch roots = lateral roots
(Primary growth)

  1. What is the function of the vascular cambium?

meristem that gives rise to new xylem and phloem
secondary growth = lateral meristem
